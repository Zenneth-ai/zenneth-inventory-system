# ZIMS Editions

## ZIMS Starter
Suitable for small businesses.

Features:
- Inventory
- Sales
- Purchases
- Customers
- Suppliers
- Reports

## ZIMS Business
Suitable for growing businesses.

Includes Starter plus:
- Multi-Branch
- Employee Management
- Payroll
- Expenses
- Notifications
- Audit Logs

## ZIMS Enterprise
Suitable for large organizations.

Includes Business plus:
- Multiple Warehouses
- Advanced Reports
- API Access
- Business Intelligence
- AI Insights
- Cloud Backup
