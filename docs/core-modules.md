# ZIMS Core Modules

These modules are available for every business type.

## Core
- Authentication
- Dashboard
- Products
- Categories
- Brands
- Inventory
- Customers
- Suppliers
- Sales (POS)
- Purchases
- Reports
- User Management
- Settings

- Cylinder Tracking
- Gas Refilling

### Baby Shop

- Product Age Categories
- Gift Registry (Future)

### Sneaker Shop
- Shoe Sizes
- Colors
- Brand Collections

### Supermarket
- Barcode Scanning
- Promotions
- Loyalty Program

### Pharmacy
- Prescription Management
- Batch Tracking
- Expiry Alerts

### Hardware
- Bulk Pricing
- Supplier Quotations

### Restaurant
- Kitchen Orders
- Table Management
- Menu Management
