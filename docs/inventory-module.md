# ZIMS Inventory Module

## Stock In
- Receive products from suppliers
- Record purchase reference
- Update stock automatically

## Stock Out
- Reduce stock after sales
- Reduce stock after cylinder exchange
- Manual stock issue

## Stock Transfer
- Transfer stock between branches
- Track source and destination branches

## Stock Adjustment
- Correct damaged or lost items
- Record adjustment reason
- Keep adjustment history

## Inventory Alerts
- Low Stock Alert
- Out of Stock Alert
- Expiring Stock Alert (Future Feature)

## Inventory Reports
- Current Stock
- Stock Value
- Stock Movement History
- Reorder Report
