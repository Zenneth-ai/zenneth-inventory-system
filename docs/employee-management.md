# ZIMS Employee Management

## Employee Profile

- Employee ID
- Full Name
- National ID / Passport
- Phone Number
- Email
- Role
- Branch
- Employment Date
- Status (Active / Inactive)

---

## Shift Management

### Shift Start

Record:

- Date
- Start Time
- Branch
- POS Terminal

### Shift End

Record:

- End Time
- Total Hours Worked
- Cash Count
- Expected Cash
- Actual Cash
- Difference
- Manager Approval

---

## Employee Performance

Display:

- Total Sales
- Total Transactions
- Products Scanned
- Customers Served
- Returns Processed
- Stock Adjustments
- Average Checkout Time

---

## Attendance

- Clock In
- Clock Out
- Late Arrivals
- Absent Days

---

## Activity Log

Every action is recorded:

- Login
- Logout
- Barcode Scan
- Sale
- Purchase
- Stock In
- Stock Out
- Price Change

---

## Manager Dashboard

Managers can view:

- Employees on Duty
- Current Shift Status
- Today's Sales by Employee
- Cash Differences
- Performance Rankings

---

## Security

Cashiers cannot:

- Delete employees
- Edit audit logs
- Change prices without permission

Managers require approval for sensitive actions.

Super Admin has full access.
