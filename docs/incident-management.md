# ZIMS Incident Management

## Purpose

Record and investigate unusual business events.

---

## Incident Types

- Missing Stock
- Damaged Stock
- Lost LPG Cylinder
- Cash Shortage
- Customer Complaint
- Supplier Complaint
- Price Error
- Barcode Error
- System Error
- Security Incident

---

## Incident Details

- Incident Number
- Category
- Description
- Date
- Time
- Branch
- Reported By
- Assigned To
- Priority
- Status

---

## Investigation

Attach:

- Photos
- Videos
- Documents
- Witness Notes
- Manager Comments

---

## Resolution

- Root Cause
- Corrective Action
- Approved By
- Resolution Date

---

## Audit Link

Every incident links to:

- Audit Log
- Employee Activity
- Sales History
- Inventory History
- Approval History

---

## Reports

Generate reports by:

- Employee
- Branch
- Incident Type
- Date
- Status
