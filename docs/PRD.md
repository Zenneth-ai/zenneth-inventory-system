 # Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Product

ZIMS – Zenneth Inventory Management System

## Tagline

One Platform. Every Business.

## Objective

Build a modern, scalable inventory and retail management platform for businesses of all sizes.

## Target Users

- Small Business Owners
- Medium Businesses
- Multi-Branch Companies

## Supported Businesses

- LPG Gas Shop
- Baby Shop
- Sneaker Shop
- Clothing Store
- Cosmetics Shop
- Supermarket
- Hardware Store
- Pharmacy
- Restaurant

## Core Features

- Authentication
- Dashboard
- Inventory
- Sales (POS)
- Purchases
- Customers
- Suppliers
- Reports
- Barcode Generation
- Barcode Printing
- Settings

## Non-Functional Requirements

- Fast
- Secure
- Mobile Friendly
- Responsive
- Scalable
- Easy to Use

## Success Metrics

- Fast product search
- Fast checkout
- Accurate inventory
- Easy reporting
- High user satisfaction
