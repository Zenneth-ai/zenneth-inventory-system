# ZIMS Sales Workflow

## New Sale Process

1. User logs in.
2. Open Point of Sale (POS).
3. Search or scan product.
4. Add product to cart.
5. Select customer (optional).
6. Apply discount (if authorized).
7. Choose payment method.
8. Complete payment.
9. Print or send receipt.
10. Update inventory automatically.
11. Record payment.
12. Log activity in audit logs.

## Supported Payment Methods
- Cash
- M-PESA
- Bank Transfer
- Credit
