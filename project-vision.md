# Project Vision

## Mission

To build a modern inventory and business management system for LPG retailers.

## Vision

Become the leading inventory management platform for LPG businesses in Kenya and East Africa.

## Core Values

- Simplicity
- Reliability
- Security
- Performance
- Scalability

## Long-Term Goals

- Web Application
- Android App
- iOS App
- Cloud Hosting
- Multi-Tenant SaaS
- AI Business Insights
- Barcode & QR Support
- SMS & WhatsApp Notifications

## Version Roadmap

### v1.0
Inventory, Sales, Purchases, HR, Reports

### v2.0
Mobile App, Delivery Tracking, Barcode Scanner

### v3.0
AI Forecasting, Business Intelligence Dashboard, Multi-company Support
